How to play

  • Place exactly one cat in every row, every column, and every colored area.
  • No two cats may touch — not side by side, and not even at a corner (diagonally).
  • Tap a tile to cycle: empty → ✕ → 🐱 → empty. Use ✕ to mark spots you've ruled out.
  • You have 3 hearts ❤️ — placing a cat that breaks a rule costs one.
  • Solve by logic, not luck. Every puzzle has exactly one answer.
