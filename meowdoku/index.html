How to grow

  • 🌹Plant exactly one rose in every row, every column, and every garden bed (color).
  • 🚫Roses need space — no two may touch, not side by side, not even at a corner (diagonally).
  • ✕Tap a tile to cycle empty → ✕ (ruled out) → 🌹 → empty. Use ✕ to mark bare dirt.
  • 🪲Plant in the wrong spot and a Japanese beetle moves in. Three and the garden's overrun.
  • 🧠Every garden has exactly one solution, always reachable by logic. Levels grow from 6×6 to 14×14.
